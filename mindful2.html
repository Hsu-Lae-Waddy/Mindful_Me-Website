A Step-by-Step Guide to Mental Wellness

🌿 What is Mindfulness?

Mindfulness means paying attention to the present moment with intention and without judgment. It helps us recognize our thoughts and feelings so we can understand ourselves better.

🕒 When to Practice?

Practice whenever you feel overwhelmed, anxious, or simply want to be present.

When Overwhelmed

  • Stress from study or work
  • Too many thoughts at once

When Anxious

  • Worrying about the future
  • Racing thoughts

When Feeling Low

  • Sadness or emptiness
  • Lack of energy

The RAIN Technique

(Recognize, Allow, Investigate, Nurture)

RAIN helps you deal with difficult emotions mindfully.

R

Recognize

What is going on inside right now? Name the emotion (e.g., "I feel anxious").

A

Allow

Let the feeling be there. Don't push it away. Accept it exists.

I

Investigate

Ask yourself: Where do I feel this in my body? What thoughts are coming?

N

Nurture

Be kind to yourself. Say "It's okay. I am here for you."

🌧️ Step-by-Step Practice

🧘 Step 1: Pause

Stop what you are doing. Take a slow breath in... and out.

🔴 Step 2: Recognize

Ask: "What am I feeling right now?" (Stress, Anxiety, Sadness). Name it.

🟠 Step 3: Allow

Tell yourself: "It's okay to feel this." Let it be there.

🟡 Step 4: Investigate

Where do I feel this in my body? (Chest, Stomach). Be curious.

🟢 Step 5: Nurture

Put your hand on your heart. Say "I am okay." Treat yourself like a friend.

🧘 Meditation Guide

Simple practice to train your mind.

What is it?

Meditation is training your mind to focus and stay calm. It is not about stopping thoughts completely, but about noticing them without getting caught up in them.

It helps you:

  • Reduce stress
  • Improve focus
  • Control emotions

🌿 Step-by-Step

  1. Find a quiet place. Sit comfortably on a chair or floor.
  2. Sit straight. Keep back straight but relaxed.
  3. Close eyes. Gently close or look down softly.
  4. Breathe. Focus on breath coming in and out.
  5. Notice wandering. When mind wanders, gently bring back to breath.
  6. Finish. Open eyes slowly. Notice how you feel.

You can practice mindfulness in small moments — walking, eating, or listening to music.
