🌱 The 90-10 Principle

10% of life is what happens to us. 90% is how we respond to it.

We cannot control everything that happens, but we can choose how we react. A calm response often leads to better outcomes than reacting with anger or frustration.

Reference: Stephen Covey's teachings on personal responsibility.

💛 How to Love Yourself

Self-love means treating yourself with kindness, respect and understanding.

It does not mean being perfect. Instead, it means accepting yourself, recognizing your strengths, and being gentle with yourself when mistakes happen.

Simple ways to practice self-love:

  • Speak kindly to yourself.
  • Take care of your physical and mental health.
  • Celebrate small achievements.
  • Set healthy boundaries.
Reference: Kristin Neff's Self-Compassion Research.

🕊️ What Is Inner Peace?

Inner peace is staying calm and balanced, even during difficult times.

It does not mean that problems disappear. Instead, it means you can face challenges without being overwhelmed by them.

Ways to develop inner peace:

  • Practice mindfulness.
  • Spend time in nature.
  • Take slow, deep breaths.
  • Focus on what you can control.
  • Let go of unnecessary worries.
Reference: American Psychological Association (APA).
